# Plugin listing copy

Ready to paste into each directory form. Endpoints are on the same Klype server and the same Klype account. Klype never publishes, schedules or sends.

## Klype Check

- **Name:** Klype Check
- **One line:** Check any draft against your saved voice and facts before it goes out.
- **Description (under 300 characters):** Paste a post, email or message and get a 0-100 score, a verdict (ready, needs changes, blocked) and specific fixes, checked against your saved voice, product facts and publishing rules. Works with any Klype account. Nothing is published or sent.
- **Example prompts:**
  1. Check this LinkedIn draft against my Klype voice before I post it.
  2. Does this cold email sound like me? What would you fix?
  3. Review this launch post for claims I have not backed up with facts.
- **Pricing text:** Free
- **Endpoint:** https://api.klype.ai/api/v1/mcp/check

## Klype Voice

- **Name:** Klype Voice
- **One line:** Draft posts in your own measured voice, then check and approve them.
- **Description (under 300 characters):** Writes posts grounded in your saved voice, product facts and buyer, checks each draft before it goes out, and records your approval. Also shows your approval record. Klype drafts only; you publish. Needs a Klype account with a plan or trial.
- **Example prompts:**
  1. Draft a LinkedIn post about what we learned from our last customer call, in my voice.
  2. Write three versions of a post on why founders should own their pipeline, then check the best one.
  3. I approve the checked draft. Record it and show my recent approvals.
- **Pricing text:** Requires a Klype plan
- **Endpoint:** https://api.klype.ai/api/v1/mcp/voice

## Klype GTM Planner

- **Name:** Klype GTM Planner
- **One line:** See what go-to-market work is due today and what has worked.
- **Description (under 300 characters):** A morning brief from your saved GTM plan, content calendar, drafts and pending approvals: what to post, draft or approve today and over the next week, plus what has performed from your imported post results. Read-only. Needs a Klype account with a plan or trial.
- **Example prompts:**
  1. Give me my Klype morning brief for today.
  2. What GTM work is due this week, and what is still not drafted?
  3. What kinds of posts have worked best for me so far?
- **Pricing text:** Requires a Klype plan
- **Endpoint:** https://api.klype.ai/api/v1/mcp/planner
