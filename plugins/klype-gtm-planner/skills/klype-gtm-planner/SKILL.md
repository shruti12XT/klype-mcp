name: klype-gtm-planner
description: See what go-to-market work is due today and what has worked.
---

Use when the user asks for a morning brief, what to post or do today, this week's GTM plan, or what has performed. Call list_workspaces, then get_todays_plan with the user's time zone, and get_performance_insights for what has worked. get_workspace_context gives the voice and facts behind the plan. Requires a Klype plan. Read-only.
