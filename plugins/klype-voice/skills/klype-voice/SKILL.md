---
name: klype-voice
description: Draft posts in your own measured voice, check them and record your approval.
---

Use when the user asks to write, draft or rewrite a post or message in their voice for a business they have in Klype. Call list_workspaces and get_workspace_context first and follow that voice and those facts only. Use draft_post, then review_draft, and call approve_draft only when the owner says they approve. list_approvals shows the record. Requires a Klype plan. Never publish or send.
